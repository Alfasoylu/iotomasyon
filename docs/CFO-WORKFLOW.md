# Persistent CFO working loop — 2026-10-04

Implemented on the existing application/database connection without migrations or new provider credentials. Owner-facing `/cfo/calisan` reads durable research items, questions, approval status, reported outcomes and a success heartbeat. Existing CFO questions and notebook remain the persistent memory. No private business records, original uploads or notebook text are fixtures or documentation.

## Working behavior

- The existing daily XML (02:00 UTC) and Trendyol (06:00 UTC) cron jobs run the CFO after their response, even if their source is disabled or empty. Both use the existing fail-closed CRON_SECRET gate. No additional Hobby cron job slot is required. Successful bank/Entegra uploads, balance updates, XML/Trendyol syncs and question answers additionally schedule evaluation with Next.js `after`. A manual ADMIN/CFO_WRITE action runs the same loop. After-response execution is best effort within the platform duration; daily cron retries missed events. This is daily plus event-driven operation, not a continuously resident or hourly worker. Upgrading to Pro is not required for this release.
- Each cycle loads the reviewed financial sources, existing costs, stock/receivables, active notebook, settings and previous questions. It uses an advisory transaction lock across observation and persistence; concurrent cycles return `already_running`. Preview deployments cannot write workflow records.
- Unique Unicode/case SKU aliases preserve digits and punctuation; exact matches win and ambiguous aliases fail closed. Financial cost and SQL weight lookups use the same uniqueness rule. Raw identifiers and business tables are not rewritten. Channel ingestion freshness, actual canonical financial-source freshness and XML inventory freshness are separate. A fresh native channel does not make stale canonical financial observations current.
- Explicit no-reorder language in Product.privateNote restricts procurement, separately from catalog activity and liquidation. Listing packages and verified dummy stock do not become stock-based procurement candidates. Missing cost does not mean unwanted stock. Case variants of notebook confidence tags are accepted; custom confidence tags remain unverified.
- The deterministic planner detects critical stock cover, excess stock, below-floor observed pricing when the canonical financial source is current, cash-floor risk and growth measurement gaps. It creates research items with evidence, blockers and nullable financial impacts. Stock quantities are explicitly illustrative 60-day scenarios, not confirmed supplier orders. Open orders, inbound coverage, profitability and own-cash constraints remain explicit blockers. Missing inbound matches never mean no goods are on the way: incomplete coverage suppresses the order-quantity scenario. Existing import lead times and batch/line minimums are included as conditional constraints, without assuming every product is imported. Supplier terms cannot be invented.
- Before asking about cost it searches existing TRY/USD/import cost fields, notebook references and all non-cancelled previous questions. A recorded cost becomes reconciliation work; a previous answer becomes research, not another question. Truncated/unavailable notebook or catalog context prevents claiming an exhaustive cost search. New questions have stable ids. The current cycle maintains a five-question active budget across existing open questions and new candidates; demand ranks missing product costs, while the debt lock deprioritizes supplier research. Older questions are retained as a paginated backlog, without deleting answers or changing manual priorities.
- Answers are stored as unverified context, with an audit entry. Merely reading an answer does not mark it financially applied or change product costs. Changes and references to attachments remain review work. Arbitrary text, SQL and commands inside notes/answers are never executed.
- Proposal revisions are hashed. Approvals re-read sources, require matching revision and a recent observation, and have recorded actor/time. Materially changed approved proposals require new review. A disappearing signal does not count as successful implementation. Rejection and completion require a reason/result. Import-related rejections and reported completions remain closed even when urgency changes. Other rejected risks stay rejected unless the risk escalates to a higher priority; routine day-to-day changes do not repeatedly ask for the same decision. Event routes have explicit duration budgets for after-response evaluation. Approval records acceptance of a proposal; it does not trigger payments, supplier orders, marketplace edits, new loans or financial balance changes.
- Goals use the existing configured monthly USD target and dated settings exchange rate. Incomplete revenue yields unknown target progress; unknown contribution profit remains null. Total recorded debt is read from the existing cfo_servet.borc source; if unavailable it stays null. Card debt is explicitly not a substitute for total company debt. Goal observation changes are preserved in the audit trail, without being presented as verified realized gains. Reported outcomes are user statements, not automatically verified realized gains.

## Operating checks and remaining scope

The success heartbeat is written only after the durable cycle commits. Failures record a fixed diagnostic without raw SQL, credentials or financial text. UI displays last success/failure and never equates deployment with a successful live business run. The coding environment has no production credential/session, so authenticated execution is confirmed through the application UI or the next scheduled cron, not by bypassing auth.

This release is an autonomous deterministic internal research/decision workflow. It is not a general-purpose LLM employee, external market researcher or a reliable prediction of future profit. It cannot complete unverified supplier research merely by repeating a template. Provider-based reasoning, verified procurement plans and automatic execution need additional independently tested capabilities. Existing release/financial acceptance flags and frozen comparisons remain unchanged.

Validation uses synthetic PGlite tables: real persistence and stable ids, repeat runs, answer/note matching, SKU collisions, null financial outputs, explicit product policy, approval expiry/revision changes, result guards and audit records. Typecheck, lint, build and unauthenticated page/API/cron gates are part of CI.

Technical delta lives here and CHANGELOG-CFO-WORKFLOW.md. The existing large PDKS/CHANGELOG documents previously rejected by automatic privacy review are not republished.

It stores a private object reference and serves downloads through ADMIN/CFO_READ/EXECUTIVE_READ authentication with attachment disposition and no-store headers. An unavailable/public bucket blocks uploads; answer text remains saved. It does not create buckets, change storage permissions or publish files. Legacy references are confined to the configured project and bucket; the application no longer links directly to them. Existing exposure through a legacy public bucket is not claimed repaired by a code deployment. Synthetic tests reject public storage, arbitrary URL hosts and path traversal.

The protected panel displays that diagnostic and refreshes the failure record after a failed manual attempt. Legacy failures retain their timestamp without inventing a cause.

Replaced per-item and per-answer database round trips with bounded JSON recordset writes within the existing transaction. Stable keys are deduplicated before batching; answer markers, unverified notes, audits and heartbeat remain atomic. Answer context is written only when its observed answer timestamp still matches. A failed batch does not advance the last success or mark an answer financially applied.

A new real PostgreSQL/Prisma integration test exercises the complete cycle, advisory locking, repeat runs, actual parameter encoding, rollback and recovery. The latency fixture persists 80 work items within a two-second transaction budget with no more than six simulated remote requests. CI creates an isolated synthetic localhost database; only the unrelated vector embedding fixture column is represented as text, with no production schema changes. The production cause and successful rerun must still be confirmed through the owner panel.

## Focused cycles, journal and debt policy

Each successful cycle now begins by reading its durable notebook heartbeat and saved work outcomes before loading financial sources. New/changed answers are read and remain explicit verification work until a result is reported; reading an attachment reference does not certify its contents. Focus rotates through cash/debt, sales, cost/profit, stock/future orders and growth. Critical risks and answer follow-ups stay visible alongside the selected topic. All observations remain refreshed; rotation bounds the displayed working agenda, not the underlying source scans. Completed/rejected work is excluded from the focus, without erasing its history. Repeated unchanged inputs increment a stagnation counter and advance the topic instead of multiplying questions.

Every committed cycle appends a human-readable notebook journal with summary, saved work, findings, proposed actions, missing/waiting inputs and the next topic. The agenda and heartbeat commit atomically with answers, questions and work records. Failures append a safe diagnostic journal when the database is reachable and preserve the last successful continuation. Recent journals have a dedicated section in the notebook and are excluded from the bounded human-knowledge context, so repeated machine notes cannot crowd out company knowledge.

The questions page shows the first five urgent/high-priority questions and a collapsible backlog paginated in groups of twenty, with actual total counts. The latest twenty answers distinguish context-read from financially verified/applied. Stable question ids, previous answer searches and an active budget prevent another bulk question run.

New purchase orders, including new drafts, require authoritative recorded total company debt strictly below TRY 5,000,000 and complete, fresh bank/card/loan balances. The gate is checked inside serializable write transactions; draft confirmation and an unplaced confirmed order moving to placement are checked too. Existing placed shipment/receipt handling continues. Forecast dates and card-only debt cannot satisfy this gate. While closed, CFO products are saved as future-order candidates with nullable illustrative quantities and a forecast-date field; the cycle never creates a supplier order. Explicit no-reorder and virtual-stock exclusions remain effective.

The conditional debt-date model reads a historical thirty-day sales window anchored before the last imported sales day, current stock, cautious velocity, inbound quantity/arrival/coverage, fixed expenses and the payment calendar. It releases paid inventory cost as cash rather than reporting it as profit, uses conservative fully costed channel cash contributions, delays receipts to the declared settlement term, caps sales at finite available stock and excludes future purchases/new borrowing. A protected owner budget records tax/fee-inclusive monthly interest, other monthly outflows, cash reserve and paid-stock attestation; it is a dated user declaration valid for seven days, not measured profit. Operating costs are reserved before additional principal repayment. Loan/card principal is not counted twice as an operating expense. Customs liabilities are recognized as debt settlements only when the recorded debt source, active import amounts and one linked calendar event agree; ambiguous taxes/customs block the date.

Insufficient sales/cost/inbound/balance/budget inputs yield a null date with specific blockers. A funding gap or insufficient stock over the recorded calendar horizon yields no reachable date. Conditional dates remain scenarios and never authorize actual orders. The model assumes the declared budget is complete, fixed daily costs/velocity and all available surplus assigned to debt, and exposes these assumptions. It does not guarantee future revenue or classify unverified free-text answers as financial facts.

Validation adds synthetic priority/backlog/rotation/journal tests and strict debt-threshold, settlement-delay, finite-stock, inbound-arrival, unknown-interest and funding-gap cases. The real Prisma/PostgreSQL complete-cycle regression remains required. No schema migration, extra Hobby cron, production data fixture or new credential is introduced.


## Existing import planner integration

`/cfo/kazananlar#ithalat` is the single destination for import and future-order planning. Each cycle reads existing `cfo_order_line`/`cfo_order_batch` rows including non-active statuses, optional row/batch notes, `cfo_urun_karar` decisions/reasons/expiry and the existing import-summary view. Row answers carry their scope and entity key into the cycle, even though those legacy columns are outside the generated Prisma model. Structured ALMA/rejected/cancelled statuses prevent a new candidate; BEKLE preserves waiting; existing pending or placed rows prevent a second SKU candidate. Case/Unicode aliases use conservative decision matching, so conflicting aliases cannot silently bypass a rejection. Expired decisions remain in history and cease blocking after their date. Missing/truncated planner sources close candidate creation while other CFO work continues.

Incomplete future candidates are durable workflow records rendered inside that same planner with nullable quantities and conditional dates. Existing native party rows and their amounts are preserved. Candidates do not become `cfo_order_line` quantities or affect native batch totals until procurement terms are verified; no transport mode, supplier purchase price or confirmed quantity is invented. The former separate list on `/cfo/calisan` is replaced with a planner link. The planner remains visible even when no monthly winners have been frozen. Current owner decisions are checked when rendering, so a rejected/waiting candidate cannot remain an active suggestion while a cycle is running; it is still available with its decision controls in history. The authoritative fresh total-debt gate also replaces misleading ready/date labels on native batch cards while debt is at/above the threshold or sources are stale.

Native rows and new candidates share the existing ITHALAT_SATIRI answers and product-decision controls. A PLAN_NOTU field permits a general row note even when no derived question exists. Owner answers/decisions and decision removal schedule a Hobby-compatible after-response evaluation. Product decisions/removal commit atomically with their audit entry; generic failure logs no longer include owner notes or raw database errors. Free text is read as evidence and review work, never parsed as executable instructions or silently applied to money, quantity, debt or orders. Import-related workflow rejections and reported completions do not reopen merely because priority or inventory changes. Every cycle records its planner review/destination in its continuation and journal.

Validation adds synthetic SQL fixtures for legacy notes/statuses, active/expired and conflicting-alias decisions, native-line deduplication, persisted candidates, unchanged native quantities, source gaps, row-answer context and rejection under increased urgency. The real PostgreSQL/Prisma full-cycle fixture includes legacy planner tables and row-answer metadata. No schema migration, new cron slot, external supplier commitment or private business fixture is introduced.
